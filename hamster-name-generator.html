Common questions

Hamster naming FAQ

What names fit a tiny hamster?

Pip, Button, Bean, Crumb and Peanut are compact options that match a small pet.

Are food names good for hamsters?

Food names are popular because they match a hamster’s size and round shape, but any clear name can work.

Can I rename a hamster?

Yes. Use the new name consistently during calm, positive interaction.
