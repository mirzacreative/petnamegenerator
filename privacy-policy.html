Privacy Policy

Read how Pet Name Generator handles browser storage, email contact and future analytics or advertising changes.

Pet Name Generator is a static website. It does not require account registration and does not operate its own user database.

Browser storage

The generator uses session storage to remember recently shown names and reduce repetition during the current browser session. It uses local storage only when you save favorite names. You can clear both through your browser settings. The site does not receive the saved values.

Email contact

If you email mirzaatif146@gmail.com, your message is handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving it. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics or advertising service is included in this release. If either is added later, this policy should be updated before deployment to identify the provider, data use, cookies and available controls.

External links

External services may apply their own privacy policies. Review those policies before sharing information.
