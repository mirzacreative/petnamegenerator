Common questions

Fish naming FAQ

Can fish have funny names?

Yes. Fish names can be playful because recall training is not the main concern.

What should I name a blue fish?

Ocean, Indigo, Cobalt, Tide and Reef are clear color or water-inspired options.

Should aquarium fish have a theme?

A shared theme can make multiple fish easier and more enjoyable to name.
