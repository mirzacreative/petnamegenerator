Cookie and Storage Policy

Learn which browser storage features Pet Name Generator uses and how to clear them.

This release does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Session storage

Recent generated names are stored temporarily in the browser session to reduce repetition. The data normally clears when the session ends.

Local storage

Favorite names are stored locally when you select the star button. The data remains in your browser until you clear favorites or browser storage.

Your controls

You can clear favorites through the generator and manage site data through your browser settings.
