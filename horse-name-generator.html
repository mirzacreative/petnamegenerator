Common questions

Horse naming FAQ

What is a barn name?

A barn name is the short everyday name used around the stable, even when the horse has a longer registered name.

Are strong names only for stallions?

No. Strong and myth-inspired names can suit mares, geldings and stallions.

How should I test a horse name?

Say it clearly at a distance and check that it remains easy to understand around normal stable noise.
