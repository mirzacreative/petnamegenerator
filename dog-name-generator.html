Common questions

Dog naming FAQ

What dog names are easiest to train with?

Short, distinct names are convenient during training. Avoid choices that closely resemble common cues or another household name.

Can I use a human name for my dog?

Yes. Human names work well when they are easy to say and feel natural for the family.

How many names should I shortlist?

Keep three to five finalists, then test each one aloud in normal situations before deciding.
