Common questions

Bird naming FAQ

What are good names for small birds?

Bright, short names such as Pip, Sunny, Kiwi and Wren are easy to repeat and match many small birds.

Can I name a bird after its color?

Yes. Color names are memorable, especially when combined with personality or song.

Should two birds have similar names?

Distinct names reduce confusion for owners, especially when calling or discussing each bird.
